Quiz Bank & Score Tracker

Each session has 5 questions — asked before the concept and after. Must score ≥ 80% (4/5) to advance. Scores are recorded here after each session.

Quiz rule: Questions test understanding, not memorization. If you score below 80%, the session repeats. There are no shortcuts.

Quiz Design Principles

Bad question (memorization): "What command starts a container?"

Good question (understanding): "A container writes to /data with no volume mounted. What happens to that data on docker rm, and why?"

Questions must require the student to reason about what the tool is actually doing, not recall a flag.
